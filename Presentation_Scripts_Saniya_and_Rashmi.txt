## Part B: Part That I Developed (Saniya’s Short Code Walkthrough — ~35 Seconds)

**[Screen Action: Switch to VS Code showing `src/rag/source_manager.py`, `src/rag/vector_index.py`, and `src/rag/hybrid_retriever.py`.]**

> **Saniya:**
> "Now let’s briefly look at the backend modules I developed inside `src/rag/`:
> 1. **Document Ingestion (`file_parser.py` & `source_manager.py`)**: I built the parser and source manager that extract text from PDFs, CSVs, TSVs, and notes, split them into clean paragraph chunks, and tag each chunk with its filename and chunk number (`Filename#ChunkID`) for citations.
> 2. **Dual Indexing (`vector_index.py` & `bm25_index.py`)**: I indexed the chunks into both an in-memory **FAISS** multilingual vector index (supporting English and Hindi) and a stop-word-filtered **BM25** keyword index.
> 3. **Hybrid Retrieval & Guardrail (`hybrid_retriever.py`)**: Finally, I combined FAISS and BM25 using **Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF)** in under 17 milliseconds, and added a confidence threshold gate that blocks out-of-domain queries before they ever reach the LLM—guaranteeing zero hallucinations. Thank you!"
